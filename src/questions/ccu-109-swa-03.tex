\begin{pastexam}[label={px:ccu-109-swa-03}]{中正資工所甲組 109 學年度 第 8 題}
（問答）

(4\%) Please transform this expression from the infix to the postfix.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item (2\%) a*(b+c/d)-e
\item (2\%) (a+c)*b+d*e
\end{enumerate}
\end{pastexam}

\begin{pastsoln}
答案：(a) \texttt{a b c d / + * e -}；(b) \texttt{a c + b * d e * +}。

用資結書虛擬碼~6.8 的堆疊轉換（資結書第~6.3.3~節）：運算元直接輸出；左括號推入；右括號把堆疊
彈到左括號為止；運算子先把堆疊頂端優先序\textbf{大於或等於}自己的運算子彈出（左結合），再推入
自己。 \texttt{*}、\texttt{/} 的優先序高於 \texttt{+}、\texttt{-}。 讀完後把堆疊剩下的依序彈出。
下表的堆疊從底寫到頂。

\textbf{(a)} \texttt{a*(b+c/d)-e}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{cllc}
\toprule
讀入 & 動作 & 輸出 & 堆疊 \\
\midrule
\texttt{a} & 輸出 & \texttt{a} & \\
\texttt{*} & 堆疊空，推入 & \texttt{a} & \texttt{*} \\
\texttt{(} & 推入 & \texttt{a} & \texttt{* (} \\
\texttt{b} & 輸出 & \texttt{a b} & \texttt{* (} \\
\texttt{+} & 頂端是 \texttt{(}，推入 & \texttt{a b} & \texttt{* ( +} \\
\texttt{c} & 輸出 & \texttt{a b c} & \texttt{* ( +} \\
\texttt{/} & \texttt{+} 比 \texttt{/} 低，推入 & \texttt{a b c} & \texttt{* ( + /} \\
\texttt{d} & 輸出 & \texttt{a b c d} & \texttt{* ( + /} \\
\texttt{)} & 彈出 \texttt{/}、\texttt{+}，丟掉 \texttt{(} & \texttt{a b c d / +} & \texttt{*} \\
\texttt{-} & \texttt{*} 比 \texttt{-} 高，彈出；推入 \texttt{-} & \texttt{a b c d / + *} & \texttt{-} \\
\texttt{e} & 輸出 & \texttt{a b c d / + * e} & \texttt{-} \\
結束 & 彈出 \texttt{-} & \texttt{a b c d / + * e -} & \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
檢查：原式是 $(a \times (b + c / d)) - e$。 後序式從左讀，\texttt{c d /} 算出 $c/d$，加上 $b$，再乘
$a$，最後減 $e$，和原式相同。
