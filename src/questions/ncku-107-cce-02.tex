3. Which of the following statement(s) is (are) true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)]
\item Quick sort and merge sort both apply the divide-and-conquer approach to solve the sorting problem.
\item Quick sort spends large effort in dividing a sequence into two parts while merge sort spends large
effort in combining two unsorted sequence into one sorted sequence.
\item Both of the two algorithms are stable.
\item The expected time complexity of merge sort is O(nlogn) which is faster than quick sort in the
worse case.
\end{enumerate}
\end{pastexam}

\begin{pastsoln}
這份考卷和成大電機所 107 學年度的「資料結構」是同一份（兩系所共用），完整詳解見歷屆試題~\ref{px:ncku-107-ee-ds-02}。

答案：(A)(B)(D)。 (B) 照出題的意思判為對；照字面（合併的其實是兩個已經排好的半段）則答 (A)(D)。
\end{pastsoln}
