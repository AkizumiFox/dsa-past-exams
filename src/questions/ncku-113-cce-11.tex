\begin{pastexam}[label={px:ncku-113-cce-11}]{成大電通所 113 學年度 簡答題第 2 題}
（簡答）

(5 pts) Show the postfix expression for the equation (A\% B)* (C-D) / (E +F) .
\end{pastexam}

\begin{pastsoln}
答案：
\[
\texttt{A B \% C D - * E F + /}
\]

\texttt{\%}（取餘數）和 \texttt{*}、\texttt{/} 同一個優先順序，都是由左到右結合；\texttt{+}、
\texttt{-} 較低。 照資結書虛擬碼~6.8 的中序轉後序：運算元直接輸出；左括號推入堆疊；右括號
一直彈出到左括號；運算子進來前，先把堆疊頂端應該比它先算的運算子彈出（優先順序較高，或同級而在左邊）。 逐個符號追蹤：
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{cll}
\toprule
符號 & 堆疊（底 $\to$ 頂） & 輸出 \\
\midrule
\texttt{(} & \texttt{(} & \\
\texttt{A} & \texttt{(} & \texttt{A} \\
\texttt{\%} & \texttt{( \%} & \texttt{A} \\
\texttt{B} & \texttt{( \%} & \texttt{A B} \\
\texttt{)} & （空） & \texttt{A B \%} \\
\texttt{*} & \texttt{*} & \texttt{A B \%} \\
\texttt{(} & \texttt{* (} & \texttt{A B \%} \\
\texttt{C} & \texttt{* (} & \texttt{A B \% C} \\
\texttt{-} & \texttt{* ( -} & \texttt{A B \% C} \\
\texttt{D} & \texttt{* ( -} & \texttt{A B \% C D} \\
\texttt{)} & \texttt{*} & \texttt{A B \% C D -} \\
\texttt{/} & \texttt{/} & \texttt{A B \% C D - *} \\
\texttt{(} & \texttt{/ (} & \texttt{A B \% C D - *} \\
\texttt{E} & \texttt{/ (} & \texttt{A B \% C D - * E} \\
\texttt{+} & \texttt{/ ( +} & \texttt{A B \% C D - * E} \\
\texttt{F} & \texttt{/ ( +} & \texttt{A B \% C D - * E F} \\
\texttt{)} & \texttt{/} & \texttt{A B \% C D - * E F +} \\
結束 & （空） & \texttt{A B \% C D - * E F + /} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
遇到 \texttt{/} 時，堆疊頂端的 \texttt{*} 和它同級，由左到右結合要先算 \texttt{*}，所以先把
\texttt{*} 彈出再推入 \texttt{/}。 這對應原式 $\bigl((A \% B) * (C - D)\bigr) / (E + F)$。
\end{pastsoln}
